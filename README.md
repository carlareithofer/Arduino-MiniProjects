# Arduino-MiniProjects and CapstoneProject

Hello everyone! My name is **Carla** and I’m no longer a newbie when it comes to electronics and Arduino! :)

I recently completed an *Arduino for Beginners* course and an electronics course, and I wanted to put what I learned into practice by building a series of small projects, alongside with a comprehensive project.

To help others who are also starting out, I’ve uploaded the projects I've worked on, along with a description of each project, a list of required components, the code, and pictures of the hardware.

I hope you find them useful, and good luck with your own Arduino journey!

***Note: all the projects in this repository use an Arduino UNO R3 Board and are programmed in C++. You will find the list with the necessary components below.***

### Necessary Components: 
Don't forget to also buy enough wires (aprox. 30 Male-to-Male wires and 10 Male-to-Female wires)
