# Arduino-MiniProjects

Hello everyone! My name is **Carla**, and I’m no longer a newbie when it comes to electronics and Arduino! :)

I recently completed an *Arduino for Beginners* course, and I wanted to put what I learned into practice by building a series of small projects.

To help others who are also starting out, I’ve uploaded the mini-projects I worked on, along with a description of each project, a list of required components, the code, and pictures of the hardware.

I hope you find them useful, and good luck with your own Arduino journey!
